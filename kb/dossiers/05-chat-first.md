# Dossier — Chat-first, low-moment, phone

**Topic:** Seconds-to-useful counsel on a phone  
**Bearing:** C5 · C3 · C6  
**Why it matters:** C5 is how C1 is supposed to feel. A folio that is beautiful and a chat that is slow is two products.

## Foundations
The ATELIER brief named chat-first, cited, for someone at a low moment. YouVersion wins on opening the book. Hallow wins on a named season. Bible Chat wins on “I can ask.” This project’s one-screen (`/ask`) is the third pattern without impersonation.

Mobile research I did **not** re-run this cycle: NN/g mobile input costs, Apple HIG 44pt, WCAG 2.2 target size. Those are **MODEL-KNOWLEDGE**. What I verified in this repo: ask textarea is 18px (iOS zoom-safe), send is 44px min-height, 988 is above the composer, cannot-do is always visible.

## Frontier
Companion apps are moving to voice and streaks. C9 / WATCH forbid a streak SKU. Voice would be a new surface and a new crisis path — do not add it this cycle.

## Live controversies
Is a chatbot the right shape for Jesus’s words, or is it inherently a false intimacy? **INFERENCE:** the honest version is one question → one saying → silence. Multi-turn “pastoral” chat is how Bible Chat and Text With Jesus get paid, and how C2/C6 break.

## Methods and limits
Usability claims below are from reading `public/ask.html` and guest.html, not from a session with a guest. Guest hour is untested with a real person.

## What the top 1% know
The first five seconds are the product. Helpline before hope. Cannot-do before the first verse. A dock, a streak, or a second saying is anxiety in chrome.

## Hardest objections
1. *Chat-first contradicts “sit first.”* **Answer:** Guest hour orders sit → ask. `/sit` now exists so that order has a URL.
2. *One screen is too thin to own a market (C1).* **Answer:** WATCH pauses ads. Thin and true beats thick and licensed.

## Implications
- Ask footer now links Need, Guest, Sit, Colophon.
- `/sit` built this cycle (Director’s Cut).
- Do not add a weekly SKU or a second meaning paragraph.
