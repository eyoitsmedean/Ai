# The Thanksgiving Group Chat: sources

Every chat bubble is a verbatim excerpt (original spelling and capitalization kept). '…' marks a trim: words were removed, never changed or added. Dividers, labels under bubbles ('Forwarded · 1784', 'Read by Abraham Lincoln'), and lines like 'muted the conversation' or the renamed conversation are interface captions, not quotes. 'Forwarded' marks a quote older than the moment it appears in, placed out of order for a joke; its real date is shown.

Checked Oct 25, 2026 through search results from the primary-source sites below (direct page fetches were blocked from the build machine). Where editions differ in spelling, the bubble was trimmed to words every edition agrees on; see the notes.

## Quotes

### 1. George Washington
> Now therefore I do recommend and assign Thursday the 26th day of November next to be devoted by the People of these States to the service of that great and glorious Being, who is the beneficent Author of all the good that was, that is, or that will be

- **Document:** Thanksgiving Proclamation, New York
- **Date:** 3 October 1789
- **URL:** https://founders.archives.gov/documents/Washington/05-04-02-0091 (manuscript: https://www.loc.gov/resource/mgw8a.124/)
- **Bubble:** …I do recommend and assign Thursday the 26th day of November next…
- **Note:** Issued at Congress's request for a one-time day, not a permanent holiday. 26 November 1789 was a Thursday, and so is 26 November 2026.

### 2–3, 8. Benjamin Franklin
> He is a Bird of bad moral Character.

> For in Truth the Turkey is in Comparison a much more respectable Bird, and withal a true original Native of America.

> He is besides, though a little vain & silly, a Bird of Courage, and would not hesitate to attack a Grenadier of the British Guards who should presume to invade his Farm Yard with a red Coat on.

- **Document:** Benjamin Franklin to Sarah Bache (his daughter), Passy
- **Date:** 26 January 1784
- **URL:** https://founders.archives.gov/documents/Franklin/01-41-02-0327 (also https://annotation.blogs.archives.gov/2020/11/25/talking-turkey)
- **Bubbles:** "He is a Bird of bad moral Character." · "…a much more respectable Bird, and withal a true original Native of America." · "…a little vain & silly, a Bird of Courage…"
- **Note:** Editions differ on "Turkey" / "Turk'y" and "though" / "tho'", so the bubbles leave those words out. The ampersand is in the letter. The letter mocks the eagle on the Society of the Cincinnati's badge; Franklin never proposed the turkey as a national symbol.

### 4. Thomas Jefferson
> I consider the government of the US. as interdicted by the constitution from intermedling with religious institutions, their doctrines, discipline, or exercises.

- **Document:** Thomas Jefferson to the Rev. Samuel Miller, Washington
- **Date:** 23 January 1808
- **URL:** https://founders.archives.gov/documents/Jefferson/99-01-02-7257
- **Bubble:** I consider the government of the US. as interdicted by the constitution from intermedling with religious institutions…
- **Note:** Miller had asked him to recommend a national day of fasting and prayer, not a thanksgiving; the caption under the bubble says so. Jefferson issued no national thanksgiving proclamations as president. Some editions print "intermeddling".

### 5–6. Sarah Josepha Hale
> This subject is to have the day of our annual Thanksgiving made a National and fixed Union Festival.

> For the last fifteen years I have set forth this idea in the 'Lady's Book', and placed the papers before the Governors of all the States and Territories

- **Document:** Sarah Josepha Hale (editor of Godey's Lady's Book) to Abraham Lincoln, Philadelphia
- **Date:** 28 September 1863
- **URL:** https://www.loc.gov/resource/mal.2669900/ (transcription: https://www.loc.gov/static/classroom-materials/thanksgiving/documents/sarah_hale.pdf)
- **Bubbles:** the first sentence in full; "…For the last fifteen years I have set forth this idea…"
- **Note:** Transcriptions differ on the quotation marks around Lady's Book, so the bubble stops before it.

### 7. Abraham Lincoln
> I do therefore invite my fellow citizens in every part of the United States, and also those who are at sea and those who are sojourning in foreign lands, to set apart and observe the last Thursday of November next, as a day of Thanksgiving and Praise to our beneficent Father who dwelleth in the Heavens.

- **Document:** Proclamation of Thanksgiving, Washington
- **Date:** 3 October 1863, five days after Hale's letter
- **URL:** https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/proclamation-106-thanksgiving-day-1863
- **Bubble:** …set apart and observe the last Thursday of November next, as a day of Thanksgiving and Praise…
- **Note:** Drafted by Secretary of State William Seward (John Nicolay, 1 April 1864; Gideon Welles's diary).

### 9. Franklin D. Roosevelt
> …I, Franklin D. Roosevelt, President of the United States of America, do hereby designate Thursday, the twenty-third of November, 1939, as a day of general thanksgiving.

- **Document:** Proclamation 2373, Thanksgiving Day
- **Date:** 31 October 1939 (the change was announced 14 August 1939)
- **URL:** https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/proclamation-2373-thanksgiving-day
- **Bubble:** …do hereby designate Thursday, the twenty-third of November, 1939, as a day of general thanksgiving.
- **Captions:** "Critics renamed the conversation 'Franksgiving'" (the nickname was widely used; who coined it is disputed, so no name is given). "Texas, Colorado and Mississippi celebrated both" per the National Archives: https://www.archives.gov/publications/prologue/fdr-thanksgiving-experiment

### 10. Congress
> That the fourth Thursday of November in each year after the year 1941 be known as Thanksgiving Day, and is hereby made a legal public holiday to all intents and purposes…

- **Document:** H.J. Res. 41, Public Law 77-379, 55 Stat. 862
- **Date:** approved 26 December 1941
- **URL:** https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-55/pdf/STATUTE-55-Pg862.pdf (also https://www.archives.gov/legislative/features/thanksgiving)
- **Bubble:** …the fourth Thursday of November in each year after the year 1941 be known as Thanksgiving Day…
- **Note:** The House version said "last Thursday"; the Senate changed it to "fourth".

### 11. George H. W. Bush
> …he will not end up on anyone's dinner table, not this guy -- he's granted a Presidential pardon as of right now -- and allow him to live out his days on a children's farm not far from here.

- **Document:** Remarks on Signing the Thanksgiving Day Proclamation, Rose Garden
- **Date:** 17 November 1989
- **URL:** https://bush41library.tamu.edu/archives/public-papers/1213 (also https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/remarks-signing-the-thanksgiving-day-proclamation)
- **Bubble:** …he's granted a Presidential pardon as of right now…
- **Note:** The official Public Papers text says "granted"; some retellings say "presented".
