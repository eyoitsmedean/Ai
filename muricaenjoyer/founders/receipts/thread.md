# Founders receipts: reply thread

Post each text as a reply under the short video, with the matching card attached.

## 1/4 (attach receipt-1.png)
Receipt 1/4: Abigail Adams to John Adams, Braintree, 31 March 1776. "Remember the Ladies," with her spelling ("favourable") kept. Full letter on Founders Online.

## 2/4 (attach receipt-2.png)
Receipt 2/4: Jefferson to Madison, 30 Jan 1787, on John Adams. "Vain, irritable" and then "all the ill which can possibly be said of him."

## 3/4 (attach receipt-3.png)
Receipt 3/4: Adams to Jefferson, 15 July 1813. Often misdated to 1812 or credited to Jefferson. It's Adams, and the capital W is his.

## 4/4 (attach receipt-4.png)
Receipt 4/4: Adams's last words on 4 July 1826, as reported by family. Jefferson had died hours earlier at Monticello. Adams never knew.
