## 1/5 (attach receipt-1.png)
Receipt 1/4: Abigail Adams to John Adams, Braintree, 31 March 1776. "Remember the Ladies." Full letter, original spelling and all, on Founders Online.

## 2/5 (attach receipt-2.png)
Receipt 2/4: Jefferson to Madison, 30 Jan 1787, on John Adams. "Vain, irritable" and then "all the ill which can possibly be said of him."

## 3/5 (attach receipt-3.png)
Receipt 3/4: John Adams to Thomas Jefferson, 15 July 1813, a year and a half into the letters they restarted in 1812. The capital W is in the original.

## 4/5 (attach receipt-4.png)
Receipt 4/4: Adams's reported words on 4 July 1826, first made public in Edward Everett's eulogy. Jefferson died at Monticello that same afternoon. Adams never knew.
