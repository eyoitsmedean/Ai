# Reviewer’s gathering — The Press

Open **`http://localhost:3000/review`** (or `/?review=1`).

This is the atelier the strategy docs asked for, built as a walkable folio — not a feature pile.

## How to sit with it

Do not tour. Turn leaves.

| Leaf | What you should feel | The idea it finishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reveal** | John 14:27 arrives word by word | Word reveal · paper, not glass |
| **Breathe** | *Peace / be / still* on a living ring | Breath prayer over Mark 4:39 |
| **Parable** | Five leaves of Luke 15, His ink only | Parable chamber |
| **Examen** | Rejoice · Review · Release · Rest | Evening pole · Lectio 365 |
| **Bless** | A name, a format (4:5 / 9:16 / 1:1), a printed card | Bless-by-name · one person, not a feed |
| **Forty** | Rooms toward Ash Wednesday 10 Feb 2027 | Named path · Hallow’s payday, without shame |

Season paper: `/review?season=lent` · `advent` · `christmas` · `easter`. The silk changes. You never have to name it.

## What must stay true

- No invented Jesus. Every crimson sentence is KJV speech.
- The chatbot is never called Him.
- A missed Forty day does not lock a door.
- 988 remains in the title page and settings.
- Chrome still leaves the room when you Sit.

## After you have walked it

The Press also lives in the main folio: rail **V**, dock **Press**, command palette (`⌘K` → “Open the Press”). Today still does the morning. The Press is the craft.
