# Review folio

Open **[folio.html](folio.html)** in a browser. That is the deliverable — a bound-book proof of Red Words, generated from the same locked catalog the Flutter app ships.

```bash
npm run folio
```

What the folio holds, in order:

1. Title and contents
2. Widget law, with a home-screen card that is only the Word
3. Title / Today / Sit as phone leaves
4. Lectio — Read, Reflect, Rest, Respond
5. Blessing card without the brand
6. Daily office clock and vespers prompts
7. Seven Days (Come through Go)
8. Twelve Seek rooms
9. The seven-morning rotation
10. Seasonal paper tokens
11. Locked identifiers and the fail-closed blank page
12. The taps that matter on a phone and a Mac

Every saying is corpus text. Nothing was written to fill a blank page.
