📖 What is this?

Ask any life question or moral dilemma, and receive a thoughtful response drawn exclusively from the direct words of Jesus Christ as recorded in the four Gospels — the passages traditionally printed in red letters in many Bible editions. Every response cites the specific verse it draws from.

ⓘ This is reflective guidance for thought and prayer — not a substitute for personal faith, pastoral counsel, or professional advice. It is offered with humility, not authority.
