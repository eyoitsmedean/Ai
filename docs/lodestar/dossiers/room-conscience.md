# Topic Dossier — Room conscience

**Bearing.** C7  
**Why it matters.** The first verse is the one a person on a bad night reads.

## Foundations

`roomFor` decides once. Retrieval and model both write from that room. `finishLetter` drops out-of-room cites and refills. Keyword-scored sayings once handed a suicidal reader “your father the devil.”

## Frontier

Eval G5 already required first citation ∈ named themes. That still passed while static Seek led Conflict with Matthew 5:44 and static Today froze a single JSON day. Theme-name gates are not first-word gates.

## Live controversies

How much “no room” should pretend. We chose honesty: “I could not tell… If you can name the weight in one more sentence, I will look again,” plus two calm verses — never a third about tribulation.

## Methods and limits

One `guessThemes` in `public/data/rooms.js`. Special lists for betrayal, danger, by-you, crisis. High-stakes and the six ordinary-drift cases are identity-tested. Meaning after a true cite is untested without a key.

## What the top 1% know

“Lost” is not Purpose. A job lost is worry; a house lost is suffering; a patient lost is grief. Affair day is Come, not forgive-not. A veteran is Shame, not Anxiety. Empty send is not “today’s word” — today’s word can be tribulation.

## Hardest objections

1. *Use the daily word for empty send — it’s prettier.* Answer: it wounded the sitting script. Two calm lines.  
2. *One composer only.* Answer: Pages has no Node. Two composers, one source.  
3. *Theme gate is enough.* Answer: it wasn’t. First-cite tests exist because of that miss.
