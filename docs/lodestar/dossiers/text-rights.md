## 1. Charge

**Paste** = the advisor displays verse wording to a person who did not supply their own Bible.

**Free US advisor** = no paid publisher license, no store IAP, household in Idaho, no confirmed United Kingdom distribution. A GitHub Pages URL on the default branch would still be a public URL reachable from the UK.

**Name** = the translation’s trademarked title (WEB, NIV, ESV, …). A public-domain *text* and a reserved *name* are different estates.

**Question this dossier answers:** of the English Gospel texts this household may lawfully and policy-consistently paste, which ones are open, which are locked, and what must `/ask` and Dean’s recording do before any public URL.

## 5. Texts that may be pasted

### 5.1 World English Bible — text public domain; name reserved

**Opened:** [eBible WEB+ copyright](https://ebible.org/engwebp/copyright.htm), [eBible IP policy](https://ebible.org/legal.php), [eBible public-domain page](https://ebible.org/publicdomain.htm), [Matthew 11](https://ebible.org/engwebp/MAT11.htm).

The copyright page states, in substance: the World English Bible is in the public domain (not copyrighted); “World English Bible” is a trademark of eBible.org; you may copy, publish, sell, give away, quote, broadcast, post, print, and teach from it; if you *change* the actual text, you must not call the result the World English Bible. The page identifies a **2020 stable text edition**. The HTML this session fetched was generated by eBible.org on **12 Sep 2026** from source files dated **12 Sep 2026**.

The IP policy repeats the split: the WEB *text* is not copyrighted; permission to use the *name* and logo is granted only for faithful copies of the translation published at eBible.org and WorldEnglish.Bible. Domain names (WorldEnglish.Bible, eBible.org, and kin) are claimed as trademarks of Michael Paul Johnson / eBible.org.

The public-domain page states that a public-domain Bible may be incorporated in apps and quoted in any amount, and that a public-domain work may still have a name protected by trademark law. It also notes UK letters patent on the printed KJV as a *different* restriction that “have no effect in most of the world.”

**Matthew 11:28 (only WEB Gospel verse opened; only WEB verse this dossier quotes):**

> Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest.

That wording is on [eBible Matthew 11](https://ebible.org/engwebp/MAT11.htm), confirmed this session. Do not quote other WEB lines from memory on a public page.

**Trademark versus text (the disconfirm this charge asked for).** Copyright and trademark are not the same right. Dedication of the WEB *wording* to the public domain does not dedicate the *name*. An advisor may store and paste the official wording in full. It may call that wording “World English Bible” / WEB only if it is a faithful copy. A modernized, shortened, or model-paraphrased line must not be labeled WEB. This session did **not** open a USPTO registration record; the mark is **asserted by the rights holder**, not independently verified here as a live federal registration.

## 6. Disconfirmations

### 6.1 Wikipedia “500 words” versus “500 verses”

**Opened:** [Wikipedia, King James Version, “Copyright status” / “Permission”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_James_Version#Copyright_status).

That page, fetched 2026-09-14, says Cambridge “permits the reproduction of at most **500 words**” for liturgical and non-commercial educational use, with the 25% and complete-book limits. Footnote 218 cites Cambridge University Press “Bibles”, retrieved 9 October 2025.

**Disconfirmed as the operative UK quotation unit.** Against that one tertiary sentence stand:

- Yale’s current guide: **500 verses**
- Law.SE’s quotation of Cambridge’s KJV permissions page: **five hundred (500) verses**
- Cambridge’s own 2021 Family Chronicle imprint: **500 verses**
- Cambridge’s permissions request form: KJV measured in **verses**

Wikipedia’s own citation is the Cambridge Bibles page — the same class of page this session could not open (Cloudflare 403). Until that HTML is read, the conflict is: Wikipedia reports “words”; every other opened Cambridge-quoting source reports “verses.” **Treat “500 words” as an error.** Do not size `/ask`, a library, or a UK risk note in words.

### 6.2 WEB trademark versus public-domain text

The name is reserved; the wording is not. See §5.1. An advisor that paraphrases a saying and still labels it WEB is the failure mode. An advisor that pastes the official wording and labels it WEB is the permitted use the trademark owner describes.

## 7. Locked-out moderns (Dean’s lock; pages opened so the lock is explained)

Dean’s license lock stands. These texts are not candidates for a free public advisor. The pages below show *why* a gratis-quote brochure is not a license to run a Gospel retrieval system.

Repo facts used as a measuring stick (KJV verse counts, already tested): Matthew 1,071; Mark 678; Luke 1,151; John 879; Gospels 3,779; spoken 1,923. Any “complete book” ban knocks out a whole Gospel. Any 500-verse cap is smaller than the spoken library. Any 1,000-verse cap is smaller than Matthew and smaller than the spoken library.

### 7.1 NIV — Biblica / Zondervan / HarperCollins

**Opened:** [Bible Gateway NIV copyright block](https://www.biblegateway.com/versions/New-International-Version-NIV-Bible/), [HarperCollins Christian permissions](https://www.harpercollinschristian.com/sales-and-rights/permissions/).  
**Blocked:** [biblica.com/permissions](https://www.biblica.com/permissions/) — Cloudflare challenge, 2026-09-14.

Gratis shape (both opened pages): up to **500 verses**, not a complete book, not 25% or more of the host work, required notice. HarperCollins: not a commentary or biblical reference work; NIV 2011 only; “smartphone application or other digital product” is sent to the licensing department. Bible Gateway’s hosting notice is stricter still (“personal use only”; “copying or reposting on the Internet is prohibited”) — that is Gateway’s site terms, not a substitute for Biblica’s own page, which this session could not read.

A sealed spoken corpus of 1,923 verses is outside the 500-verse grant. A public advisor is a digital product.

### 7.2 ESV — Crossway

**Opened:** [Crossway permissions](https://www.crossway.org/permissions/).

Gratis shape: up to **500 verses** in print, digital, and audio, not more than one-half of any one book (or its equivalent in bytes), not 25% or more of the host work, **not** a commentary or other biblical reference work. Required notice includes: the ESV “may not be quoted in any publication made available to the public by a Creative Commons license” and “may not be translated in whole or in part into any other language.” “ESV” and “English Standard Version” are registered trademarks.

A Jesus-speech advisor that stores and retrieves Gospel sayings is, in substance, a biblical reference work plus a digital product. The spoken library exceeds 500 verses. Crossway also offers a non-commercial API — that is still a **license path**, not a free-household paste.

### 7.3 NASB — Lockman Foundation

**Opened:** [Lockman, Permission to Quote](https://www.lockman.org/permission-to-quote/).

Gratis shape: up to **1,000 verses**, not a complete book, not more than 50% of the host work, **and** “nor may more than 1,000 verses be stored in an electronic retrieval system.” Web pages and apps must carry the full copyright notice with a click-enabled lockman.org link. Marks (NASB, New American Standard Bible, lighthouse logo) are claimed as USPTO-registered trademarks.

The electronic-retrieval clause is fatal to a sealed NASB Gospel corpus. 1,923 spoken verses > 1,000. Matthew 1,071 is already a complete book and already over 1,000. Lockman’s separate AI-response allowance (up to 1,000 verses in a *single response*) is a chatbot courtesy, not a grant to ship a local corpus.

### 7.4 NRSV — National Council of Churches / Friendship Press

**Opened:** [Friendship Press licensing FAQ](https://www.friendshippress.org/pages/nrsvue-quick-faq).

Gratis shape for RSV / NRSV / NRSVue: up to **500 verses**, less than an entire book, less than 25% of the words of the host work, no changes to the text, required notice. Then the exclusion this product cannot survive:

> The Fair Use Guidelines do not apply to Phone Applications, or other New Media Platforms (web sites, apps, etc.).

Mobile apps, downloadable Bibles, and software “will require a special permission or license.” Except for NRSV-CE, “the NRSV is no longer available for new licenses or permission agreements.” A free public `/ask` is exactly a website. The gratis brochure does not cover it.

### 7.5 CSB — Holman / Lifeway

**Opened:** [CSB permissions](https://csbible.com/permissions/), [CSB FAQ](https://www.csbible.com/about-the-csb/faq/).

Gratis shape: up to **1,000 verses**, not more than 50% of the host work, not a complete book. Required Holman notice; “Christian Standard Bible” and “CSB” claimed as federally registered trademarks. The permissions form states requests that exceed 50% of the work, or that quote a complete book, **will not be approved**.

Matthew is a complete book (1,071 verses). The spoken library is 1,923 verses. A CSB-backed advisor would need a signed Holman grant Dean has not sought and has locked out.

### 7.6 The Message — NavPress / Tyndale

**Opened:** [Bible Gateway MSG copyright block](https://www.biblegateway.com/versions/Message-MSG-Bible/).

Gratis shape: up to **500 verses**, not a complete book, not 25% or more of the host work, required NavPress / Eugene H. Peterson notice (© 1993, 2002, 2018). Excess use to Tyndale (`permission@tyndale.com`). Same arithmetic as NIV/ESV: the spoken library does not fit; a digital advisor is not a church bulletin.
