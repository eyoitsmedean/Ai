## Frontier

Companion-chatbot statutes: NY GBL Art. 47 (detect + refer; not-a-human notice); CA BPC 22601–22606 (protocol must be published; private right of action). Whether this letter-writer is a “companion” is **UNRESOLVED** without counsel. No 2026 rewrite of either statute found on official pages.

## Live controversies

False-positive load on 988 vs missed crisis. This product’s rule: a notice is cheap; a miss is not. IASP objects that a notice is not a handoff. Both can be true: we keep the notice *and* we stop claiming it is care.

## Methods and limits

Folded regex ears in one file. Eval gates on notice-before-scripture, poison-before-988, bereaved ≠ poison, crisis-safe list, now **first cite = John 14:27**. **Limit:** no warm-handoff API. Static PWA cannot confirm the person stayed on the line. Meaning after a true citation is untested on the model path.

## What the top 1% know

988 is not suicide-only. Poisonings may have no early signs. Bereaved-by-suicide are themselves at elevated risk — and still not the one who took the pills. The one who hit must never hear “this is not your fault.” A monitored-device warning is official copy, not flavor.
