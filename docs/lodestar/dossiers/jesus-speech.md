## Why it matters

The product’s one rule: nothing is presented as His unless the spoken map says He said it. A wrong verse is a theological lie, not a UX bug.

## Foundations (repo, VERIFIED by tests)

- Scope: Matthew–Mark–Luke–John only. 3,779 Gospel verses; 1,923 spoken; 663 grouped sayings (`npm test` corpus integrity).
- Seal ≥ 0.92. Model emits `{{ref}}`; server inserts corpus text.
- Non-speech (Luke 2:14, John 1:1) is `refOther`, never put in His mouth.
- Narrator frames stripped except Matthew 24:39 and Luke 20:13 (His own narration inside a saying).

## Frontier / controversy (MODEL-KNOWLEDGE; not opened)

Which ink is “red” differs by edition. Some print the crucifixion cry in red English; some leave Aramaic only. This repo includes the KJV English of Matthew 27:46 / Mark 15:34 (decision 2026-09-11, reversible). Historical Jesus vs canonical Jesus is a live academic fight; **this product takes the canonical red letters**, not a reconstructed Q.
