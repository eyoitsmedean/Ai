Plan I · fastest cash

The Lamp

Get paid to judge AI-written training and GTM artifacts. Human judgment only.

$80–$120/hr listed
I

One breath. Platforms pay experienced L&D and sales people to score model output against a rubric. You already do that kind of seeing for a living. The work needs no audience, no ads, and no testimonial release.

Who pays

Expert-network / AI-eval marketplaces. The public listings you already saved: Training / onboarding / L&D Evaluator and General Sales / GTM Evaluator, advertised $80–$120/hour as of 30 August 2026.

90-day math

If accepted and supplied 5–8 billable hours a week for eight weeks: about $3,200–$7,680 gross. Modeled base used in your studio: $4,000 for a 40-hour engagement. Selection probability is unknown. Net is less after tax.

Already built

Internal readiness packet, synthetic practice, LLM-policy receipt, and a 20-minute decision page. Agents may not write real evaluations. You must.

Twelve weeks

Wk 1 · 4–10 Sep

Five truth decisions only. Years in L&D. Years in sales/GTM. Slide fluency. Outside-work / IP language. Consent to monitoring. Pursue / Park / Close.

Wk 2 · 11–17 Sep

If Pursue and written clearance is clear: apply in your own words. Availability: “up to eight hours a week, self-scheduled.” No agent-written assessment.

Wk 3–4

Interview and work trial, human-authored. Re-read the LLM policy the morning of every task. Isolate a personal device.

Wk 5–12

Bill the hours. Stop if the weekly floor exceeds eight, task supply dies, a task wants employer material in a model, or sleep fails.

First 60 minutes

Plan II · fastest yes from a human

The Room

A 75-minute high-stakes rehearsal for people who must present, panel, or defend a case.

$349 · 75 minutes
II

One breath. Not generic interview coaching. An original practice case, a mock panel, three changes, one preserved strength, and a seven-day drill plan. You already dominate rooms. Sell that, once, to people you do not meet through work.

Who pays

Enablement, customer-facing, and training people changing jobs or pitching internally — found via old classmates, church, veterans, music friends, and public posts. Never current employer staff, candidates, or clients.

90-day math

4 paid sessions = $1,396. 8 = $2,792. Optional three-session at $795 only after two clean singles. Calendar tax is real: 75 minutes live plus ~45 minutes case prep. Cap at two evening sessions a week.

Twelve weeks

Wk 2–3

Ten personal messages, not blasts. Goal: two booked diagnostics. Free 15-minute fit call, then paid.

Wk 4–8

Deliver. Ask each buyer for one referral in an adjacent company, not theirs if it overlaps work.

Wk 9–12

If four paid: keep. If fewer than two paid after 40 asks: kill. Do not discount below $249.

Plan III · highest ticket

The Handoff

One messy operating process → map, SOP, job aid, owner card, 30-day test.

$1,500 beta
III

One breath. Independent consultancies and founder-led home/property firms pay when one weekly handoff drops photos, owners, or invoices. You already wrote the package. Do not start this until The Lamp is Park/Close and you still want a client.

Scope lock

One process. ≤5 people. Two revisions. First review in 10 business days, final in 15. No software overhaul. No insurance, benefits, HR, or safety training. Standard price $2,250 only after a paid beta teaches you the real hours.

90-day math

One beta = $1,500. Two = $3,000. Your model wanted ~$108/hour at standard price over 20 hours. Cap unpaid sample build at 10 hours. After two paid units, stop below $60/all-in hour, >20% revision churn, or >15% admin.

Finish the fictional premium home-maintenance case: estimate → schedule → field → proof → invoice. Stop at 10 hours even if ugly.

Wk 2–5

20 qualified asks from public Google listings (not the employer book). One 20-minute diagnostic. No free custom build.

Wk 6–9

Deliver one beta or park. Invoice 50% to start, 50% on acceptance.

Wk 10–12

If accepted and hours held: a second beta or reprice to $2,250. If 40 asks and zero paid: kill.
