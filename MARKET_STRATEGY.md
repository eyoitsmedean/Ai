## 1. The answer

Red Letter is a quiet page for the words Jesus spoke: four rooms each morning, an Advisor that answers only in red letters with every line verified against the KJV corpus, a blessing you can send to one person, and paths that follow the church year. It is a companion, not a Bible reader, and it is free.

The market says three things, all `[verified] (retrieved)` this session:

1. **Reading is owned and free.** YouVersion passed one billion installs on 17 November 2025 and charges nothing. Do not compete on reading.
2. **Paying happens for a named habit, not for a library.** Hallow earned roughly $40M net in 2025 on an annual subscription and a 40-day Lent path; its Ash Wednesday spike is clockwork (263K downloads on 18 Feb 2026) but its Lent month has shrunk three years running (2.0M → 1.5M → 1.2M). The season is real; the category is not growing.
3. **The red-letter niche is no longer empty, but nobody owns it.** Three products now sell "the words Jesus spoke" (§3). The two App Store listings have no ratings to display. The differentiator that remains is trust: verified speech, no invented verse, words never locked, and a human handoff when someone is in danger.

What we refuse is settled (`[decision]`, `CLAUDE.md`): never lock his words, never a weekly price, never a model speaking as Jesus or God, never Paul or the whole Bible, no feed, no second product architecture, no merge with Red Words, no paid ads as a first move.

The category thesis from v1 still stands and is kept verbatim `(prior-worker)`:

> *YouVersion made the Bible accessible. Hallow made prayer a subscription habit. Bible Chat made Scripture conversational. The open throne is making the red words of Jesus the product — guidance, encouragement, and daily presence from what He actually said.*

| Red-letter toggles on full Bibles; KJV clones with red text | Feature, not a product | — | — | `(prior-worker)` |

**What this changes.** Red Letter Cards is the closest thing to our Advisor that exists: red letters only, one struggle, reverent scarcity, AI that will not speak for God. It validates the concept and removes the claim that the idea is unoccupied. It does not remove the gap, because none of the three does all of the following, which this repo already does `(in-scope)`:

- quotes only from a verified corpus, with every line checked against the red-letter map before it is shown (`lib/scripture.js`, `test/`);
- leaves the words free (Cards and Quotes of Christ paywall the reflections; we paywall nothing);
- follows the church year with named paths (Seven Days, *Watch with me* for Advent, Forty for Lent);
- sends a blessing to one person instead of posting to a feed;
- opens a human-help notice before scripture when someone types a 2 a.m. sentence (`data/crisis.js`, evaluated in `eval/`).

**Verdict `[inference]`.** The white space is now "trusted red letters, free, in season." Not "red letters" alone. Speed matters less than it did in August; trust and the calendar matter more.

## 4. Who it is for

**Primary reader** `(prior-worker, kept)`: the anxious believer who wants Jesus, not noise. 25–54, Protestant or de-churched, already opens YouVersion or faith Instagram, carries anxiety, grief, a marriage, a job, a child; wants application, not seminary. U.S. first, then English-speaking countries.

What the evidence adds `[verified] (retrieved)`:

- Evangelical Protestants are the heaviest users of scripture apps: 39% use an app or site that helps them read scripture, 18% daily (Pew Research Center, *Online Religion*, 2 June 2023 — [report](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2023/06/02/use-of-apps-and-websites-in-religious-life/)).
- Among U.S. evangelicals online, Facebook is used by 81% and YouTube by 71%, Instagram 45%, TikTok 35%; under-30s lead with YouTube 85%, Instagram 68%, TikTok 63% (Infinity Concepts / Grey Matter Research, *Helpful or Harmful? Evangelicals and Social Media*, Dec 2024 — [PDF](https://www.infinityconcepts.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Helpful-or-Harmful.pdf)). v1 attributed these numbers to Pew; the source is Infinity Concepts.
- Bible use rose among Millennials from 30% to 39% between 2024 and 2025 (ABS 2025, above) — the age band of the primary reader.

Deprioritised, unchanged from v1: Greek/Hebrew study users (Logos), kids-first (YouVersion Kids), full Catholic prayer stacks (Hallow).

## 5. What we compete on, and what we refuse

| We compete on | Because | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verified speech: every quote from the KJV corpus, checked against the red-letter map; no invented verse, ever | One fabricated quote ends the brand (v1 risk, kept) | `lib/scripture.js`, `test/`, `eval/` |
| His words free | The right to be shared is earned by not charging for the sentence | `LAUNCH.md` |
| The church year | The calendar launches the category; Hallow's whole engine is one season | `lib/year.js`, `data/paths.js` |
| One blessing to one person | The share that is not a screenshot of an app | share card, Bless |
| A quiet page | Paper, one accent colour, no feed | `DESIGN.md` |
| Human help first when it is needed | Trust line that lets a family sleep | `data/crisis.js`, 988 in-product |

| We refuse | Recorded | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locking scripture, any tier | `[decision]` ≤ Aug 2026 | v1 §6 proposed limiting free chat to 3–5/day and locking the offline library behind Plus. Retired: it contradicts the decision. |
| A weekly price | `[decision]` ≤ Aug 2026 | Bible Chat's pattern (§2) |
| A model speaking as Jesus or God | `[decision]` ≤ Aug 2026 | Red Letter Cards makes the same promise; it is table stakes now |
| Paul, the whole Bible, a full library | `[decision]` ≤ Aug 2026 | YouVersion owns reading |
| A second product architecture; native store apps for Red Letter; a merge with Red Words | `[decision]` 4–5 Sep 2026 | v1 §6 and §8 proposed React Native / Expo and a native phase 1. Retired. Native lives in Red Words. |
| Licensed modern translations, a "toggle translation" feature | `[decision]` corpus is KJV 1769, public domain | v1 proposed budgeting for translation licences. Retired. v1 §9–10 said "WEB corpus"; this repo is KJV. |
| A Lifetime tier | `[proposal, rejected]` | Never lock the words makes it meaningless; it was a v1 test idea with no evidence |
| Paid ads as a first move; the meme account as distribution | `[decision]` 5 Sep 2026 | `studio/ninety/` |

Messaging pillars, kept verbatim `(prior-worker)`: **Clarity** — "Not every verse — His words." **Comfort** — anxiety, fear, loneliness answered from the Gospels. **Trust** — verbatim, cited, no invented Jesus. **Simplicity** — one minute or one conversation. **Identity** — for people who love Jesus more than religious noise.

Risks, kept verbatim from v1 `(prior-worker)` except the licensing line: YouVersion will always own "read the Bible" — don't fight that. AI trust: one fabricated quote can destroy the brand — hard retrieval and citation over creative paraphrase. Category ethics: aggressive trials and hard cancels earn one-star reviews and App Store scrutiny. Money is real but concentrated: most Bible apps make about $0; winners own a sharp emotional niche and distribute relentlessly.

## 6. Money — parked `[proposal]`

Nothing in this section is switched on, and nothing in the 5 Sep – 4 Dec 2026 window turns it on (`[decision]`, `studio/ninety/`). It is written down so January-you does not reinvent it.

| Plan | Price | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | The words, lectio, Seek, Seven Days, *Watch with me*, the curated Advisor, the blessing, the journal. Always. |
| Year | $59.99 (or $69.99 to match Hallow) | Hallow's annual is $69.99 `[verified]`; Red Letter Cards is $39.99; Quotes of Christ $44.99. One decision a year; trust. |
| Month | $9.99 | For people who will not annual. Never lead with it. |
| Week | do not sell | `[decision]` |

What a paid tier could hold: the live LLM Advisor (the curated Advisor stays free), extra seasonal paths beyond the free ones, atelier extras. Trial shape: seven days of the live Advisor, converting after someone has kept a week, not after they have hit a gate (`LAUNCH.md`). Target economics from v1 (free → trial ≥ 4–8%) are `(prior-worker, unverified)` category folklore and should not be planned against until there is one real month of data.

## 7. Distribution — when the time comes

The 90-day rule is organic only: ten humans who will open a link, a blessing that leaves the phone, three quiet vertical clips filmed and maybe never posted (`studio/ninety/PLAYBOOK.md`). What follows is for after 4 December, and it is a plan, not a commitment.

- **Meta cannot target religion.** Detailed targeting for religious practices and groups was removed on 19 January 2022 (Meta developer blog — [announcement](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2021/12/08/updating-metas-detailed-targeting-options/)) `[verified]`. Plan around lookalikes of real users, faith-adjacent interests (Bible study, worship music, parenting, mental wellness), and creative that carries the signal.
- **Where the reader already is** `[verified]`: Facebook (81% of online evangelicals) and YouTube (71%); Instagram and TikTok for the under-30s (§4). Start Meta; add TikTok when there are three real faces, not a logo (`LAUNCH.md`).
- **Apple Search Ads** later, on intent: "words of Jesus," "red letter Bible," "Jesus quotes," "daily Jesus," "lectio divina" `(prior-worker)`. Two App Store products now use "red letter" in the name (§3); those keywords are no longer empty. `[inference]` They may already bid on them; that was not checked.
- **The calendar** `[verified]` (`lib/year.js`, run 2026-09-07): Advent Sunday **29 November 2026**; Ash Wednesday **10 February 2027**; Easter **28 March 2027**. Hallow's data says the download spike lands on Ash Wednesday itself and the revenue lands six to eight weeks later. Film in January; be live before the door opens.
- **Measures that matter** (`LAUNCH.md`, kept): Day-1 lectio completed; Day-7 of a path; blessings sent per weekly active; Advisor used; annual attach if money is ever on. Faith apps live or die on the first week.
