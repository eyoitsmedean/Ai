# Launch — what the next 90 days actually are

Research (August 2026): Hallow made ~$40M in 2025 on **one named season** (Pray40 / Lent) plus an **annual** subscription. Ash Wednesday 2026: 263K downloads in a day. Bible Chat wins **downloads** with TikTok “talk to Scripture,” then burns trust with $4.99/week. YouVersion owns reading, is free, and will not leave a red-letter hole for you if you stay a Bible reader.

**Nobody owns “live daily life guided only by the red letters” as a premium brand.** That is the farm.

Do not compete with YouVersion. Do not copy Bible Chat’s weekly paywall. Copy Hallow’s *named path* and Bible Chat’s *I can ask him something* — then stay quieter and more honest than both.

## The loop that pays the family

1. **TikTok / Reels / Stories** — a face, a feeling, one red sentence, “I sat with this.” Not a feature tour.
2. **They open.** Onboarding is one page. Then **lectio**. Then **Day 1 of Seven Days**.
3. **They ask.** Advisor answers from the Gospels *with no API key*. If they feel heard, they return.
4. **They send a blessing.** That is your YouVersion verse-image. One person, not a feed.
5. **Day 7.** Invite them to begin again, or to sit through Advent / Lent when the calendar turns.
6. **Lent** is the payday season. Build the 40-day path *before* Ash Wednesday. Hallow’s calendar *is* their launch.

## How you know it is working

| Signal | Healthy |
| --- | --- |
| Day-1 lectio completed | > 40% of new opens |
| Day-7 of Seven Days | > 18% of those who started |
| Blessing sent | > 8% of WAU |
| Advisor used | > 25% of WAU |
| Paid (when you flip it on) | Annual attach, not weekly |

Where the numbers come from: each device keeps its own ledger (Room settings → *The ledger*) and, only if the reader turns on *Share anonymous counts*, sends completed days once as plain totals with no identifier. `GET /api/signal/summary?days=30` on the API host answers the first four rows with their denominators. Day-1 lectio and Seven completion are exact; blessing and Advisor are per active device-day, because no id is sent and "WAU" cannot be unique devices without one. Read it every Monday; the target column is in the response.

If Day-1 lectio is low, the onboarding is too long. If Day-7 is low, the days are not a story. If blessings are low, the button is still furniture.
