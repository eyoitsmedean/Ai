## The loop that pays the family

1. **TikTok / Reels / Stories** — a face, a feeling, one red sentence, “I sat with this.” Not a feature tour.
2. **They open.** Onboarding is one page. Then **lectio**. Then **Day 1 of Seven Days**.
3. **They ask.** Advisor answers from the Gospels *with no API key*. If they feel heard, they return.
4. **They send a blessing.** That is your YouVersion verse-image. One person, not a feed.
5. **Day 7.** Invite them to begin again, or to sit through Advent / Lent when the calendar turns.
6. **Lent** is the payday season. Build the 40-day path *before* Ash Wednesday. Hallow’s calendar *is* their launch.
