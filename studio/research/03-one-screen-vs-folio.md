# A3 — One-screen vs five-room folio

**Question.** Should Red Letter become a single Ask page, or keep Today / Seek / Sit / Advisor / Journal?

**Connection.** Prompt 4’s filled example said “chat-first advisor.” The recorded room decision (brief + lock + `public/index.html`) is five rooms. Notion 11 Sep specifies one screen as the *advance*, folio stays paper, no launch.

## Answer

Build the one-screen as a **WATCH surface** (`/ask`). Keep the five-room folio. Do not delete rooms. Do not make the one-screen the default `/` this session.

## Deeper explanation

These are different jobs.

| Surface | Job | Risk if it becomes the only thing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-screen | One ask, one saying, one implication, a cannot-do, a stop | Loses daily rhythm, lectio Sit, Seek rooms, Journal keep |
| Folio | A bound book: morning word, seek a need, sit, ask at length, keep | Too much chrome for a crisis or a 30-second ask; easier to wander into launch energy |

Dean’s original “chat-first” example was already corrected on 6 Sep to “Advisor is a room.” The 11 Sep page is not a silent reversal of the rooms; it is a constraint on *what may be shown as the product when Advent wants a URL*, and a spec for the smallest honest screen.

Replacing the folio tonight would discard accepted work (church year, Sit, Seven Days, room chips) to avoid understanding it. The commission forbids that.

## Disagreements

- Chat-first example vs locked rooms — already flagged in `studio/briefs/red-letter.md`.
- Notion “exact three blocks” example omits “What that might mean today” in one late section, while the rules section includes it. We implement **all four** blocks named in the rules + one-screen copy heading.

## Practical implication

`/` remains the folio. `/ask` and `/one-screen.html` are the WATCH page. A quiet “folio” link sits in the colophon. Service worker precaches the new page (cache `v15`).

## Decision

**Both, with roles.** One-screen for the recovered 11 Sep intent; folio preserved.
