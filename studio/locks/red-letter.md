## Project Brief

* **Project:** Red Letter — a quiet reading room for the words Jesus actually spoke. Not another Bible app. A daily companion constrained to the red letters of Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John, typeset like a small press.
* **Primary outcome:** A world-class, production-usable folio that a tasteful human would keep open. Complex in the making, almost invisible in the using. Family livelihood is attached to whether people return tomorrow.
* **Audience:** Someone who loves Notion because it feels like good paper. They notice tracking. They would close an app that shouts and keep an app that breathes. They are not shopping for streaks, celebrity pastors, or an AI Jesus.
* **Context:** Repo `github.com/eyoitsmedean/Ai`. Dean Ray. The product already exists. Continue it. Do not start a second app. Do not invent a native Flutter rewrite inside this lock — that is **Red Words**, a sibling product with its own lock.
* **Existing assets:** `DESIGN.md`, `LAUNCH.md`, `MARKET_STRATEGY.md`, `README.md`, `public/` app, `server.js` Advisor harness, spoken KJV corpus, curated Today/Seek fallbacks, share cards, crisis 988, church-year paper.
* **Required deliverables:** The running app, not a memo about the app. Browser-verified UI. Tests green. Scripture verified against the corpus. Advisor never types a verse — only `{{John 14:27}}` placeholders the harness fills.
* **Constraints:** His words stay free. No weekly paywall. No invented Scripture. No stock doves. No gamified holiness. Chrome whispers. Crimson is speech, not brand paint.
* **Non-goals:** YouVersion clone. Bible Chat weekly casino. Therapy, medical care, or pastoral counseling. Accounts or payments unless Dean explicitly asks. Touching Storyframe or OneDigital.
* **Success:** A stranger can Open → Read → Sit → Ask → Keep → Print without a tutorial. Offline Today and Seek still work. Advisor is honest without an API key. Crisis path is visible. The page looks like a bound book.
* **Brand:** Folio, not feed. Paper `#F4EFE4`, ink `#1B1610`, crimson `#8F1D1D` for His speech only. Fraunces / Source Serif 4 / Instrument Sans. Printer’s device: italic crimson R in a hairline circle.
* **Risk:** Scripture accuracy is sacred. Fabricated verses are a ship-stopper. Fake “production-ready” claims are a ship-stopper.
* **As-of date:** 4 September 2026.
