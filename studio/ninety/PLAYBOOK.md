# I · The Compound

## Doctrine

The business that already pays the house is the 90-day business. You run enablement for a twelve-week producer cohort. Week 2 is a live block on **Tuesday 8 September**. Later live blocks land on weeks 4 / 6 / 8 / 10. Same Monday twenty. No new slides.

You already wrote the method. This book does not invent a twelfth module. It makes Q4 *legible* so November-you can say what you now own.

**Clean room.** Names, accounts, recordings, and performance live only in the approved company system. This folio stores shapes, not rows.

## The method you already named (public-safe)

Monday, twenty minutes, work hours:

1. Choose five real opportunities in the approved system.
2. One buyer-centered first sentence each. Not five quotes.
3. Rehearse **VERIFY · USE · STOP** — verify the brief, use one relevant fact, ask one buyer-centered question, then stop talking.
4. No new slides. No after-hours chasing.

In the live block they stay on their feet:

- Seven-second call: permission, one fact, silence after the ask.
- Four minutes, no demo.
- Close: one date, one artifact, one owner.

**Proof, only after a named sales leader agrees:** observed attempt → one task correction → observed re-attempt within seven days. The leader owns both observations. You do not chase proof in Notion.

## First-sentence mill (steal the shape)

A first sentence is not a tagline. It is the buyer hearing themselves.

```
You have been [situation they already feel],
and the next meeting will [cost they already fear],
unless someone [the one job you can do with them this week].
```

Tests before it leaves your mouth:

- Could they say the sentence back without your product name in it?
- Does it name a date, a person, or a decision — not a platform?
- If you deleted the last clause, would it still be true?

Refuse: invented ROI, stacked adjectives, “just circling back,” any number you cannot source in the approved system.

## Private outcome ledger (never in this repo)

One line per used artifact, in the company system or a paper card in your bag:

```
Date:

Used by: [role, not name]
What changed: they attempted / they asked a better question / they booked a date
What I will not do next time:
```

Six lines by 4 December is a Compound. Sixty lines is a relapse into dashboards.

## Seat sentence (write in November, not tonight)

Fill once, in private, after the cohort has a body:

> In August I was the person who ______.  
> By December I am the person they call when ______.  
> In 2027 I want the seat that ______.  
> I will not get there by building a night business that copies this job.

## Kill

- Household gate red → Compound shrinks to “keep the job safe.” That still counts.
- Building a parallel enablement product, course, or LinkedIn grind at night → contamination. Stop.
- Storing a prospect name in personal Notion → delete the row. The method stays. The name goes.

## What it is not

A consulting brand. A side hustle. A prompt pack for sale. Anything that uses a client, a deck, or a method from the building.

# II · October

## Doctrine

October is a birthday and an arrival. The household does not need a third operating system. A designed freeze is the only way the job, Advent, and the chapter survive.

Your own constitution already says: protection lasts at least thirty days after birth, and longer if recovery needs it. No date restarts optional work. Rest needs no receipt. Either adult may pause the weekly window without explanation.

This book does not add checkboxes to that. It names the *few* things that must exist *before* the month starts, then shuts the factory.

## Before October (September family project — one sitting)

Do these on a Sunday, ten minutes, then close Notion.

1. **Who has the boys if she goes in.** A name. Not a protocol. Not a hospital plan.
2. **Tuesday work blocks on a calendar you will actually see.** Week 2 is 8 September. Weeks 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 belong on the work calendar, not a second brain.
3. **His three:** early, or cake at home after. Pick. Mark a wall or a paper. Do not build a party OS.
4. **Breakfast as a date** stays a date or it moves. It is not a metric.
5. **One bag that makes a 2 a.m. exit boring.** Clothes for her, clothes for you, charger, snacks for the boys’ person. If the bag already exists, do not reopen the list.

Anything medical, legal, or account-numbered does not live in a project folio. You already know where those belong.

## The freeze (1–31 October)

**Allowed**

- Required work, in work hours.
- Sleep, food, the boys, her, the little.
- One weekly household window: Confirmed / Not confirmed / Not needed.
- Night work that makes tomorrow morning lighter: a bag, a meal, a text, a load of laundry.

**Forbidden**

- Optional sprints. New agents. “Just a research hour.”
- New pages, hubs, databases, rankings.
- Advent feature work. Memoir *unless* writing is the thing that steadies you — one sitting, then stop.
- Factory conversations. Applications. Domains. Ads.
- Opening this folio to add a sixth book.

## The night test (say it out loud)

> If I do this tonight, is tomorrow morning easier for her or the boys?  
> If no, it is a product. It is the wrong night.

## Sunday, three minutes (keep your existing close)

1. Did the week get lighter?
2. Is the household window still true?
3. Continue the freeze, or — only if both adults say yes — name one November craft.

Park creates no reminder. Close creates no successor.

## Done by 4 December

You are still a person the household can stand. The birthday happened. The arrival had a named adult for the boys. You did not start a sixth hub.

## Kill

Any no, or uncertainty, on the family gate pauses everything else. That already is your rule.

## What it is not

A busy-parent app. A content series. Proof that agents can hack a household.

# III · One Chapter

## Doctrine

The book is the thing only you can make. The thesis is already in the forward: tell the story of the best people you served with, so other veterans feel less alone and the people who love them understand a little more.

The skeleton you already made has two holes the forward promised and almost no scenes: **coming home**, and **the apartment and the dog**. The second is Chapter 11, Post EAS, status *Needs writing*. Your last instruction to yourself (16 August) was: stay in the seconds after the noise. Do not write the decision.

This 90 days does not start a memoir business. It finishes **one** hole.

## The pick (make it once)

Default, because you already named it as the next move: **The apartment and the dog.**

If that night is the wrong night for your body, write **Coming home** instead. Not both. Write the pick on a card. Do not make a new Notion page for the pick.

## Hard rules (from your own companion)

- Never invent a name, rank, crash, or KIA.
- Prefer a concrete room, sound, or body over a lesson.
- Keep your sentences. Cut the model’s.
- If two voices exist, flag them. Do not merge them.
- Park sci-fi, the day job, and SWOT. Wrong book.
- No TED-talk ending. Brotherhood first, then what it cost. Humor in the squadron, quiet on the losses.

## The scene (Chapter 11)

You already wrote the clean sentence in 2017. The true way has noise in it.

Stay in the apartment. Years ago. Not today. No dog in the house now.

**Write:** something has already left your hand; the sound is still in the room; the dog is backing up like the floor had changed. Empty hand — did it stay open, or close? Did you look at the dog, or at what broke? The first small, stupid, human thing you did next.

**Do not write:** the decision to learn inward. Nepal. Nathan. Counseling. The meaning. The afterword.

The decision can be earned later. This chapter is the gap.

## Eight sittings · 45 minutes · September and November

October is off unless writing steadies you — then one sitting, then stop.

| # | When | Job | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sep, night 1 | Open the chapter. Read your 2017 sentences. Write 200 words that stay after the noise. No summary. | A sound is on the page. |
| 2 | Sep, night 2 | The hand and the dog. Only those two bodies. | You can see what the dog did. |
| 3 | Sep, night 3 (optional) | The first small stupid thing after. | The scene has a next physical action. |
| — | October | Off. | — |
| 4 | Nov, night 1 | Read aloud. Cut every sentence that explains. | Half as long, same proper names. |
| 5 | Nov, night 2 | One pass for invented causality. If you do not remember, write *I do not remember* and stop. | No fake chronology. |
| 6 | Nov, night 3 | Read aloud in under fifteen minutes. | A clock agrees. |
| 7 | Nov, night 4 | Give it to one trusted reader. Use the ask below. | It left your hands. |
| 8 | After they reply | Three sentences of their reaction, kept with the chapter. No rewrite unless they caught a false fact. | The 90 days can close. |

If you only get sittings 1, 6, and 7, you still have a chapter. Sittings 2–5 are craft, not permission to stall the send.

## Companion prompts (only if stuck)

Highlight the paragraph. Ask one, then stop.

- Continue this scene for 200 words. Stay in the room. No summary.
- Ask me 5 questions only I would know, then stop.
- Cut this in half without losing a proper name.
- This sounds like a eulogy. Make it a night, not a speech.

If the model writes a lesson, delete the lesson. Keep the room.

## The reader ask (copy, send once)

> I finished a chapter of the book. It is one night in an apartment, years ago. I do not want notes on market, structure, or whether it should be a podcast. I want three sentences: what you saw, where you did not believe me, and whether I sounded like I was performing. You can say you do not want to read it.

Pick a person who will tell you the truth. Not an agent. Not a publisher. Not a group chat.

## Kill

- A sitting becomes research about oral-history markets, pricing, or Veterans Day → close the file.
- The chapter starts performing for a buyer → cut it back to the night.
- You open a second hole before one reader has the first → you have left the book.

## What it is not

A memoir business. A music capsule. Therapy. A public serial. A query letter.
