Five things worth doing

A prompt was built from the life, then run. This is the output — not another catalog of businesses.

Compound October Chapter Advent Factory

Yesterday you already received ten income plans. The studio already holds five cash paths. None have a paid customer. The real 90 days are not ninety working days. September has maybe three optional weeks. October is a freeze you wrote into your own constitution. November has evenings. Call it twenty to thirty-five optional hours, total.

Anything that needs a funnel, a cohort of strangers, or a weekly content machine is a fantasy about a different season.

1 · The Compound

Make Q4 at work the business that already pays the house.

Treat the producer cohort and the artifacts you are already making as the 90-day project — not a side hustle you start after bedtime.

You said it yesterday: you are being paid to write prompts and coach sellers, and it is awesome. Week 2 of the cohort is live this Tuesday. Enablement is being rewritten as the person who can make AI change how sellers talk. That is the job you are already doing. A clearer seat inside a national firm is worth more than five local website fixes, and it uses hours you are already awake for.

The work

  • This week: five real opportunities, one buyer-centered first sentence each. No new slides. Stop.
  • September: one artifact a week a seller can use without you in the room. Private outcome list only. Nothing leaves the building.
  • October: protect the job and the household. No optional after-hours polish.
  • November: one page for yourself about the seat you now own. If a scope conversation is real, have it.

Done

A cohort that ran, a handful of used artifacts, and a sentence about 2027. Not a side-income dashboard.

Kill

If the household gate is red, this shrinks to “keep the job safe,” which still counts. If you start an enablement product at night, you have left the compound. Stop.

2 · October

Design the freeze so the other four ideas have a household to come home to.

The 90-day project with the highest life ROI is the month you already named: a birthday, an arrival, two young boys, a partner who does not need a third operating system.

Your Family page is wiser than your Venture Studio. October will punish the habit of opening Notion when the window is open. A designed freeze is not quitting. It is the only way Advent, the memoir, and the job survive.

The work

  • Before October: who has the boys if she goes in; the Tuesday work block on a calendar you will see; early birthday or cake at home.
  • October: one weekly household window, confirmed or not needed. No optional sprint. No new agent.
  • The only allowed night work: something that makes tomorrow morning lighter. If it is a product, it is the wrong night.

Done

You are still a person the household can stand. The birthday happened. The arrival had a named adult for the boys. You did not start a sixth hub.

Kill

Any no — or uncertainty — on the family gate pauses everything else. That already is your rule.

3 · One Chapter

Finish one chapter of Our War. Give it to one trusted reader. Stop.

The book is the thing only you can make. Stop turning the veteran life into a service and write the night, not the offer.

You have a spine, a companion that is not allowed to invent firefights, and three named holes. Previous plans tried to productize veteran plus audio plus motif. That is a different object. A finished chapter is evidence. Another capsule brief is not.

The work

  • Pick one hole. Coming home, or the apartment and the dog. Not both.
  • Forty-five minutes, two nights a week, September and November. October is off unless writing steadies you — one sitting, then stop.
  • Stay in the room. No TED ending. Never invent a name or a crash.
  • When it can be read aloud in under fifteen minutes, give it to one human who will tell you the truth. Not an agent.

Done

One chapter, read by one person, three sentences of their reaction. No query letter. No website.

Kill

If a sitting turns into research about oral-history markets, you have left the book. Close the file.

4 · Advent

Put Red Letter in front of ten humans by 29 November. Do not add a feature.

The product is built. The category clock is real. Your 90 days include the first week of Advent and not Lent. Ship distribution, not architecture.

Hallow’s seasons move hundreds of thousands of downloads. You will not out-celebrity that. You can own a quieter claim: daily life from the words he actually spoke. Advent is a dress rehearsal for Ash Wednesday, 10 February 2027. A rehearsal with zero audience teaches you nothing.

Red Words is a separate ship line. Do not merge them. No Mac this quarter means no store this quarter.

The work

  • September, one sitting: a real HTTPS URL. Open it on a phone as a stranger.
  • Do not add Paul, a feed, a paywall, a new shell, or a second corpus.
  • Before 29 November: twenty-eight Advent rooms, or Seven Days four times in seasonal clothes. Name the path. Film three quiet verticals. Send the link to ten people who will open it.
  • 29 November to 4 December: be a user. Count who comes back on day two.

Done

A URL a stranger can open. A named path. Ten sends. Three clips on the camera roll. A day-2 count, even if it is one.

Kill

Still redesigning on 15 November? Ship Today and stop. Buying ads before ten humans have sat is avoiding the scare of sending.

5 · The Factory

Close the idea shop. Choose one existing cash lane for after October — or choose none.

Your scarce resource is not ideas. It is nights, and you have been spending them on prompts that produce more nights.

In thirty hours you asked for 90-day income plans twice, then asked again for five ideas. Zero of the existing lanes have a buyer. Another catalog is how the factory feeds. The executive project is to starve it.

The work

  • Twenty minutes: read yesterday’s two folios once. Pick one cash lane, or write NONE through December, on This week. Do not build a new page for the pick.
  • If the pick is paid judgment on AI artifacts: freeze the readiness pack. After October, one application or one conversation. Not both. Not “to see.”
  • If the pick is a local one-fix: after October, walk into three places you already go. Look at the booking path. Say the sentence or do not.
  • If the pick is NONE: that is a finish. The other four are already a full life.

Done

A written pick dated in September. One human conversation in November, or an explicit NONE you do not reopen when an agent offers a sixth menu.

Kill

The next time you type “build me 5 plans,” close the chat. If a lane needs a logo, a domain, or a new database, it is a relapse.

The pick

If you can only run two: October and The Compound. They protect the household and the paycheck. Everything else is optional craft.

If November opens and you still have heat: Advent is the only product motion that uses something you already built and a date that will not move. One Chapter is the only meaning project that cannot be copied. Run one of those, not both, unless writing is rest.

The Factory is not a third project. It is the rule that keeps you from commissioning a fourth folio tomorrow.

Do not ask another model for five more businesses this month. You are not under-strategized. You are over-crewed. The next useful prompt is “open the URL,” or “write 200 words in the room,” or “five first sentences for Tuesday.”
