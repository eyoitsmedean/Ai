Book I

The Compound

Make Q4 at work the business that already pays the house.

You run enablement for a twelve-week producer cohort. Week 2 is a live block on Tuesday 8 September. Weeks 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 keep the same Monday twenty. No new slides. This book does not invent a twelfth module. It makes the quarter legible.

Monday · twenty minutes · work hours

  • Five real opportunities in the approved system.
  • One buyer-centered first sentence each. Not five quotes.
  • VERIFY · USE · STOP — verify the brief, use one relevant fact, ask one buyer question, stop talking.
  • No new slides. No after-hours chasing.

On their feet

  • Seven-second call: permission, one fact, silence after the ask.
  • Four minutes, no demo.
  • Close: one date, one artifact, one owner.

Proof only after a named sales leader agrees: observed attempt, one correction, observed re-attempt within seven days. The leader owns both observations. You do not chase proof in a second brain.

First-sentence mill

You have been [situation they already feel], and the next meeting will [cost they already fear], unless someone [the one job you can do with them this week].

Could they say it back without your product name? Does it name a date, a person, or a decision? If you delete the last clause, is it still true? Refuse invented ROI and “just circling back.”

Seat sentence · write in November

In August I was the person who ______. By December I am the person they call when ______. In 2027 I want the seat that ______. I will not get there by building a night business that copies this job.

Book II

October

Design the freeze so the other four have a household to come home to.

A birthday and an arrival. Your constitution already says protection lasts at least thirty days after birth, and longer if recovery needs it. Rest needs no receipt. Either adult may pause the weekly window without explanation.

Before October · one Sunday · ten minutes

  • Who has the boys if she goes in — a name, not a protocol.
  • Tuesday work blocks on a calendar you will actually see.
  • His three: early, or cake at home after. Pick. Mark a wall.
  • Breakfast as a date stays a date or it moves.
  • One bag that makes a 2 a.m. exit boring. If it exists, do not reopen the list.

The night test

If I do this tonight, is tomorrow morning easier for her or the boys? If no, it is a product. It is the wrong night.

Allowed in October: required work, the household, one weekly window (Confirmed / Not confirmed / Not needed), and night work that lightens morning. Forbidden: optional sprints, new agents, research hours, domains, ads, a sixth hub, and opening this folio to add a book.

Book III

One Chapter

Finish one hole of Our War. One trusted reader. Stop.

The forward already named the book: the best people you served with, so other veterans feel less alone and the people who love them understand a little more. Two promised scenes are almost empty. Your last instruction to yourself was Chapter 11 — stay in the seconds after the noise. Do not write the decision.

Pick the hole once

Write the apartment if you can. The scene is years ago: something has already left your hand; the sound is still in the room; the dog is backing up like the floor had changed. Empty hand — open or closed? The dog, or what broke? The first small, stupid, human thing next. Do not write Nepal, Nathan, counseling, or the meaning.

The reader ask

I finished a chapter of the book. It is one night in an apartment, years ago. I do not want notes on market, structure, or whether it should be a podcast. I want three sentences: what you saw, where you did not believe me, and whether I sounded like I was performing. You can say you do not want to read it.
