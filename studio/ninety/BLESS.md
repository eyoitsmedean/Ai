# First blessing · one person · no market
### Phone card · 14 September 2026
Bearing: C6

This is the first send. It is not the ten Advent names. It is not a streak. It is not a waitlist.

Sit Today once. After Amen **clears**, copy **this** — to one human you already love. Then stop recruiting for the night.

---

## Who

Write one name before you open the share sheet.

Not: a group chat, a market, ten people, a meme account, her as a referee, a stranger who might convert, an agent.

If you cannot name them in one breath, you are not sending a blessing. You are building a list. Close the sheet.

---

## What leaves the phone

The saying you just sat with. Not a browse of twenty-four verses. One sentence why this word is for them tonight. No URL. No “I made a page.” No ask to sit, subscribe, or reply.

Copy, send once:

```
[Name] —

I sat with this tonight and thought of you.

“[the saying you just heard]”
[citation]

No need to reply. If it is the wrong night, delete it.
```

The first Amen sheet copies the saying. It does not share a Red Letter card. Later blessings may use a card; tonight does not.

---

## What you do not send tonight

The ten-name Advent script (that is November, after a URL exists):

```
I made a quiet page for the words Jesus actually spoke.
If you want a week that does not shout, this is the door.
It is free. His words stay free.
[URL]
If it is the wrong week, delete this. No offense.
```

That script recruits. A blessing does not. Ten humans before 29 November is a later count. The first send is one.

---

## After

Four marks on paper, then household:

1. Did they get a saying, or a pitch?
2. Did the blessing leave the phone?
3. Did you feel the urge to add a second name?
4. Did you feel the urge to redesign the sheet?

A missed Tuesday is not a break. There is no streak to protect. If you did not send, name that and stop. Do not open a CRM.
