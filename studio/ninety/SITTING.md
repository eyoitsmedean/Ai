# One sitting · 45 minutes
### Phone card · 14 September 2026 · two sittings max this season

Open this. Set a 45-minute timer. Do not open the playbook first. Do not open a dossier.

October is off unless writing steadies you the way a walk steadies you — then one sitting, then stop.

---

## 0 · Gate · three minutes · any yes closes the file

Say these out loud. A yes is a finish, not a delay.

1. If I write tonight, is tomorrow morning easier for her or the boys? If no — close the file.
2. Will this sitting cost her a sleep block, or me a night that will not leave? If yes — close the file.
3. Am I opening the hole to get something off my chest, or to stay in one room? If the first — close the file. That is therapy. Not this book.

If the gate is red, the sitting happened. Write `14 Sep · gate red · no page` on the card. November is off unless a later night is clearly rest.

---

## 1 · The hole · once

Default: **the apartment and the dog** (Chapter 11). Coming home only if this night is the wrong night for your body. Not both. Not a new Notion page.

---

## 2 · The room · forty minutes

Read your 2017 sentences. Stay after the noise.

**Write:** something has already left your hand; the sound is still in the room; the dog is backing up like the floor had changed. Empty hand — open or closed? Did you look at the dog, or at what broke? The first small, stupid, human thing next.

**Do not write:** Nepal. Nathan. Counseling. The meaning. A single cause. A bow. A sentence that explains why the night mattered.

If you do not remember, write *I do not remember* and stay in the next thing you do remember. Do not invent chronology to make a lesson.

Stern as a short story. Two bodies. A sound. Stop at 45 even mid-sentence.

---

## 3 · Close · two minutes

- A sound is on the page, or the gate was red. Either is a sitting.
- Did the night leave? If it will not leave, or you do not sleep, or she lost a block — **November is off.** Clinical, not craft.
- Do not send this raw page to a group, a Discord, or an agent. One civilian later, only if a second sitting is already rest. The ask is in `PLAYBOOK.md`.

Second sitting this season (November, only if September was rest): read aloud, cut every sentence that explains, give it to one trusted reader. Not both Advent and this chapter unless writing is rest.
