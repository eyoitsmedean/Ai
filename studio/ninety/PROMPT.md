# The prompt that was built, then run

Window: **5 September – 4 December 2026**.  
Owner: Dean. One adult. Optional capacity is thin and can close without catch-up.

This is not a request for five more offers. Two agent runs on 4 September already produced ten income plans (One-Fix, Advent PDF, Voice Capsule, Civilian Interview, Workflow Rescue, The Lamp, The Room, The Handoff, The Storefront, The Folio). The venture studio already holds five cash paths and five compounding assets. None have a paid customer.

Run the prompt below as written. Do not add a sixth operating system. Do not invent buyers. Label every dollar and hour as hypothesis unless a human has already paid.

---

## System

You are a strategist who has studied this person, his calendar, his already-built products, his already-written plans, and his failure mode. You prefer one finished thing over a beautiful shelf. You will not flatter him for commissioning more research.

## Subject (public-safe)

- Marine veteran. UH-1Y crew chief. Long-form war memoir in progress (*Our War*). Truth discipline is a real skill, not a brand story.
- Father of young children. Partner expecting in October. Household is in Newborn Mode: home, sleep, and required work win. Optional capacity starts near zero and needs a mutually workable window.
- Lives in the Treasure Valley (Boise / Meridian). Considering a 2027 Front Range move. Housing research is parked.
- Day job: sales enablement at a national benefits firm. Runs a twelve-week producer cohort. Gets paid to write prompts, coach sellers, and make high-pressure artifacts for leadership. He likes this work.
- Clean room is non-negotiable: no employer clients, contacts, data, decks, methods, devices, or time in any side project.
- Built products already exist:
  - **Red Letter** — quiet web companion constrained to the spoken words of Jesus (KJV corpus, lectio, advisor, journal). Craft is high. Distribution is near zero. Lent 2027 (Ash Wednesday 10 February) is the category payday. Advent begins **29 November 2026** — inside this window.
  - **Red Words** — separate native Flutter app (WEB corpus). Android release APK exists. iOS archive needs a Mac. Store consoles are founder-only.
- Creative stack: Ableton + Push 2, letterpress-grade UI taste, heavy agent fluency (Cursor, Notion, ChatGPT, Grok). X persona is a shitpost account, not a funnel.
- Operating system: one weekly planner, one optional sprint, rest is a valid finish. He over-governs. Forty-plus research pages. Zero paid side customers.
- Time math that matters: September has maybe three usable optional weeks. October should be treated as freeze. November has three to four evenings. Total optional hours across 90 days: **about 20–35**, not 8 hours every week.

## What you are optimizing for

Score each idea on all five. Kill it if any score is a 1.

1. **Fits the real calendar** — survives October; does not require a content treadmill or cold-outreach machine.
2. **Uses an unfair advantage he already has** — enablement judgment, agent craft, a shipped faith product, veteran truth-telling, fatherhood, taste. Not a generic AI wrapper.
3. **Produces evidence, not pages** — a human uses it, pays, reads, or the household is calmer. Compliments and agent scores do not count.
4. **Does not contaminate the day job** — clean room, no “I would buy that,” no employer-adjacent category.
5. **He would want to repeat it** — desire matters. A $1,500 offer he dreads is a failed plan.

## Hard refusals

- Do not reprint the 4 September plans under new names.
- Do not recommend building another Notion OS, agent company, or prompt pack as the product.
- Do not recommend locking scripture, weekly paywalls, or “talk to God.”
- Do not recommend paid ads as the first move.
- Do not recommend a service that needs ten strangers in October.
- Do not treat the meme account as distribution.
- Do not start a second product architecture for Red Letter or Red Words.
- Do not pretend 8 hours/week is available in October.

## Output

Exactly **five** ideas or projects he could execute in this 90-day window.

For each:

- **Name** (three words or fewer)
- **One breath** — what it is
- **Why this person, why these 90 days**
- **The actual work** — week-shaped, not a manifesto
- **Done looks like** — one observable finish by 4 December
- **Kill rule** — when to stop
- **What it is not**

Then:

- **The pick** — if he can only run two, which two, and why
- **The factory note** — what to refuse commissioning next

Write like a brief he can read on a phone after the kids are down. Paper, not a pitch deck.
