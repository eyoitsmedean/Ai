# Sunday freeze list
### Ten minutes · once before 30 September · then close Notion
Bearing: C4

Paper. Not a new page. If the bag already exists, do not reopen it.

---

## Five spoken lines (replace, do not grow)

1. **Labor night.** Who has the boys the night she goes in — a name, not a protocol.
2. **Firstborn week.** Who owns the older boys’ first week (mornings, school or childcare, one thing that is not the baby). If one adult can do both 1 and 2, write that.
3. **Tuesday blocks.** Weeks 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 on a calendar you will see. Pocket sentence: if you cannot safely run a live block, you send it. The job shrinks to “keep the job safe.” No after-hours polish in October.
4. **Birthday.** Early, or cake at home after the arrival. Mark a wall. This is firstborn-protection, not a party OS.
5. **The bag.** If it exists, stop. Breakfast as a date stays a date or it moves — not a metric.

**One extra name, still not a sixth page:** who covers the house so the working parent can take **one 4-hour sleep block** in the first two weeks. Fathers in the opened studies do not nap; sleepiness rose at 12 weeks while hours slightly improved (Mellor 2015, **SOURCE-REPORTED** this cycle via full article opened 14 Sep).

---

## Night test (say it)

> If I do this tonight, is tomorrow morning easier for her or the boys?  
> If no, it is a product. It is the wrong night.

Do **not** make her confirm a weekly “household window.” That is monitoring she should not owe.

---

## October (1–31)

**Allowed.** Required work. The household. Night work that lightens morning. Writing only if it steadies you like a walk — one sitting, then stop (`SITTING.md` gate).

**Forbidden.** Optional sprints. New agents. Research hours. Domains. Ads. A sixth hub. After-hours Compound polish.

**November is not pre-authorized.** Advent send or chapter only if both adults say the window is green **and** you are not in the 6–12 week sleepiness peak. Default: watch only.

---

## Done by 4 December

The household can stand you. The birthday happened. Arrival had a named adult **and** the firstborns had a named week. No sixth hub.
