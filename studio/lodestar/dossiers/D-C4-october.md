# D-C4 · Designed October / father capacity
Bearing: C4 · Depth: D4 · Changed: `FREEZE.md`, folio `#october`, `NIGHT.html`

**Why it matters.** If two books run, October is one of them. A freeze that only names labor night and a bag still leaves the older boys and his sleep as unowned work — the work Daminger calls anticipation and monitoring.

## Foundations

Father sleep in the first twelve weeks is short and interrupted, and it does not bounce at week six. Mellor & Van Vorst 2015 (*Workplace Health & Safety*; Sage page opened 14 Sep this session): N=221; aggregate sleep 5.5 h at 6 weeks, 5.9 h at 12 weeks; daytime sleepiness 40% → 64%; fathers with DS 12% more likely to report a workplace near-miss at 6 weeks, 14% at 12 weeks. The model did **not** reliably distinguish reported work accidents or road incidents. **SOURCE-REPORTED** (full Sage HTML opened 14 Sep). CDC adult floor remains ≥ 7 h (**SOURCE-REPORTED**, 11 Sep archive; not re-opened today).

Cognitive household labor is four moves — anticipate, identify, decide, monitor — and women in Daminger’s 35-couple sample did more of it, especially anticipation and monitoring (ASR 2019; Behavioral Scientist companion opened 14 Sep). Birthday-party planning in that essay is her list, then his “opinion.” **SOURCE-REPORTED.** Daminger is not a firstborn study. Transfer to “firstborn week” is **INFERENCE**.

## Frontier / controversies

- Sleepiness rose at 12 weeks while hours slightly improved (Mellor). A freeze that assumes “it gets easier at six weeks” is false for this sample.
- Subsequent-father load: Mellor’s 2015 *article* does not break out parity in the paragraphs opened today. A Griffith thesis abstract (Mellor) reports subsequent fathers higher on conflict/sleepiness/fatigue — **SOURCE-REPORTED**, different document. Do not collapse them.
- No RCT of “Sunday lists.” The object is capacity design, not a clinical intervention.
- “Confirm a weekly household window” adds monitoring she should not owe (Daminger). Retired from the folio this cycle.

## Methods and limits

Self-report sleep and near-miss; Australian sample; no actigraphy in Mellor 2015. Varin 2022 actigraphy (6 months, ~401 min, six consecutive hours on ~57% of nights) was opened 14 Sep by Scholar, not re-fetched this pass. Nielsen 2025 / NSF 10 Jun 2026 / Philpott / Kuo: cited in the 14 Sep sitting notes; **not re-opened this pass** — do not rest new numbers on them.

## What the top 1% know

The scarce object is not a better bag. It is **named ownership of the children who are already here**, plus one sleep block that does not require her to be the project manager of his rest. Fathers in Mellor do not recover sleepiness on the same curve as hours. November is not a prize for surviving October.

## Hardest objections

1. “Lists don’t change sleep.” They don’t. They stop *unowned* firstborn week and a sleep block that never gets spoken. Answer: the list is speech, not treatment.
2. “Daminger isn’t about October.” Correct. She is about not adding monitoring. That is why the weekly-window confirm left the folio.
3. “I can do labor night and firstborn week.” Write that. Do not assume it.
4. “Pre-authorizing November is hope.” Hope that spends her bandwidth. Default: watch only.
5. “Near-miss ≠ accident.” True (Mellor). The freeze still protects the job: send the live block if you cannot safely run it.
