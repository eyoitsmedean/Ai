# D-C7 · Closing an idea shop
Bearing: C7 · Depth: D4 · Changed: `REFUSAL.md`, KNOWLEDGE C6, folio `#factory`

**Why it matters.** The original ask was five ideas. The factory is the relapse of that ask. A dated `NONE` that does not reengage is empty withdrawal.

## Foundations

Webb & Sheeran 2006 (*Psychological Bulletin* 132(2); PubMed 16536643 opened 14 Sep): **47 experimental tests**. Medium-to-large intention change *d* = 0.66 → small-to-medium behavior change *d* = 0.36. **Not** “47% of intenders act.” That sentence is retired. **SOURCE-REPORTED** (abstracts + White Rose record; full PDF not paginated here).

Kautonen, van Gelderen & Fink 2015, *Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice* (DOI 10.1111/etap.12056, page opened 14 Sep): of 371 wave-1 positive intenders (Austria/Finland, 2011–2012, one-year window), 139 (37%) took start-up action and **232 (63%) did not**. **SOURCE-REPORTED.** Distinct from Kautonen et al. 2013 *Applied Economics* (Finland 2006–2009, longer gap) — do not merge the papers.

Wrosch, Scheier, Miller, Schulz & Carver 2003, *PSPB* (PDF + PMC review opened 14 Sep): adaptation to an unattainable goal is **disengagement and reengagement**, separable capacities. Disengage without reengage leaves distress. **SOURCE-REPORTED.**

Bieleke, Keller & Gollwitzer 2021 (if-then review PDF opened 14 Sep) and Bieleke et al. 2022 *Motivation and Emotion* (page opened 14 Sep): if-then plans create “instant habits” that can impair disengagement from a dead goal. **SOURCE-REPORTED.** So the factory if-then must name a *living* book (freeze, compound, sitting), never shop labor.

## What the top 1% know

Closing a shop is not a slogan. It is a two-step on one dated card: let the founder-fantasy go, and write the next real act in a book that still has hours. A menu from an agent is the cue. The then-clause is a close, then a Compound sentence or a freeze line — never “rank the lanes.”

## Hardest objections

1. “63% is Europe, postal, self-report.” Yes. Direction is enough: intention ≠ a company.
2. “If-thens are the gold standard — why forbid one?” Because the gold standard can automate the shop. Write the then-clause on a living book.
3. “NONE is quitting.” NONE is Wrosch disengagement. The reengagement line is the other half.
4. “Sheeran 47% is what everyone cites.” Everyone cites it wrong. 47 is the *k* of tests.
5. “Lamp / One-Fix keep a lane warm.” Allowed if the rumination is not “founder this year.” Diagnostic is on the card.
