# Mastery brief · A sitting that can refuse
Bearing: C5 · Kind: craft

## Who is world-class (written sources; no video)
- **Mary Karr** — *The Art of Memoir* (chapter “Why Not to Write a Memoir”); interviews opened 14 Sep (*The Writer*, *The Cut*, NPR page). Voice; therapy first; body cost; evidence that reverses the story you wanted.
- **Tobias Wolff** — *This Boy’s Life* 30th-anniversary intro (LitHub 2019); Alta 2024. Stern as a short story; no single cause; stop when overcome.
- **Jonathan Shay** — communalization (excerpt only).
- **Sayer et al.** — the clinical case *against* unguided nights (2015 PDF; 2019 abstract).

Popularity is not the proof. The proof is a practice that will not open a hole on a short-sleep night.

## World-class looks like
A container smaller than the wound. A gate that counts as a sitting. No bow. One civilian later, not a chorus. November off if the night will not leave.

## Difference that makes the difference
Amateurs protect the art. The best protect the household *and* the art, in that order, and will date a refusal.

## Process (from the sources, not from taste)
1. Distance in years is not distance in season (Karr).
2. Speak the three questions aloud. Any yes closes.
3. One room. After the noise. Empty hand.
4. Ban cause, Nepal, Nathan, counseling, meaning.
5. After-care is clinical, not craft.

## Checklist
Gate spoken · hole picked once · 45-minute timer · no invention of chronology · card marked if red · no Discord.

## Practice loop
At most two sittings this season. Second only if the first was rest.

## Traps
Twelve sittings “to find voice.” Sending raw pages to a group. Borrowing WET’s safety reputation. Calling avoidance what is actually a red gate.
