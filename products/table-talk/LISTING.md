# Table Talk: listing kit

Everything needed to sell **Table Talk: 52 conversation cards from the words of Jesus** as a printable download today, and as a printed deck later.

## Where to sell

| Platform | Use it for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Etsy (digital download)** | Main shop | Buyers already search Etsy for "conversation cards" and "Christian printables". Instant delivery, no stock. |
| **Payhip or Gumroad** | Second shop and email list | Lower fees, can give away a free sampler and collect email addresses, and can sell a church/group license. |
| **The Game Crafter** | Printed deck, made to order | Prints poker decks one at a time and ships them for you. Our card images are already 825 x 1125 px at 300 dpi, which is its poker card size. |
| **MakePlayingCards** | Printed deck for yourself, gifts, or a small batch to sell at church events | Good quality at low quantities. Its poker template is slightly different in size, so check it against our images before uploading. |

Start with Etsy and Payhip. Add the printed deck once a few reviews come in and you have made the tuck box (see the last section).

## Description

```
Good talk around the table, one card at a time.

TABLE TALK is a printable deck of 52 conversation cards. Each card pairs words Jesus spoke in the Gospels, from the King James Version, with one open question that anyone at the table can answer, from age 8 to 88.

A few of the cards:
• "Come and dine." (John 21:12)
  What is your favorite meal to share with people you love, and why?
• "I have called you friends" (John 15:15)
  What makes someone a true friend?
• "Be not afraid, only believe." (Mark 5:36)
  What helps you feel brave when you are nervous?
• "Peace, be still." (Mark 4:39)
  Where do you go, or what do you do, when you need to feel calm?

FOUR SUITS, THIRTEEN CARDS EACH
• Love: friendship, kindness and caring for each other
• Courage: fear, hope, honesty and taking a brave step
• Peace: rest, worry, home and the gift of a calm heart
• Grace: mercy, second chances, generosity and joy

HOW TO PLAY
Shuffle, draw a card, read the saying aloud and then the question. Anyone can answer, in any order. Pass is always a fine answer. Two how-to cards are included in the deck.

WHAT YOU GET (instant download)
• Print-and-cut PDF, US Letter, 12 pages
• Print-and-cut PDF, A4, 12 pages
  9 poker-size cards per sheet (2.5 x 3.5 in) with crop marks. Each fronts page is followed by its backs page, lined up for double-sided printing.
• 55 card images (54 faces and the back) at 300 dpi with bleed, as a ZIP, if you would rather order a real deck from a print-on-demand card shop

GOOD FOR
Family dinners, road trips, small groups and Bible studies, youth groups, Sunday school, couples, grandparents' visits, and a thoughtful gift.

DETAILS
• Every saying is checked word for word against the red-letter text of the King James Version
• Questions are open-ended, never quizzes, and work for mixed ages
• Print on card stock (heavy paper around 80 to 110 lb cover works well) and cut along the crop marks; a paper trimmer makes it quick
• Choose "flip on long edge" when printing double-sided

PLEASE NOTE
This is a digital download. No physical item will be shipped. Colors may vary slightly by printer.
For personal and household use: print as many copies as your own family needs. Please do not share or resell the files. For a church or group license, send a message.
```

## Good to know

- **Scripture copyright.** The King James Version is in the public domain in the United States. In the United Kingdom it is still under Crown patent (Letters Patent held through the Cambridge University Press), so selling printed KJV text to UK buyers is a small legal grey area. Most sellers simply sell through US-based platforms; you can also leave the UK out of a physical-deck shop.
- **Fonts.** IM Fell English and Literata are under the SIL Open Font License, which allows embedding them in PDFs and images you sell. The fonts themselves are not sold or shipped as font files.
- **License for buyers.** Personal and household use only; no sharing, reselling or uploading the files elsewhere. A church or group license is sold separately. Etsy's digital listings support this note in the description; Payhip lets you attach a license text to each product.
- **Tone.** The deck is for conversation and is non-denominational. It is not a teaching curriculum, and it is not a substitute for counseling.
- **Quotations.** Every saying on the cards is checked against the red-letter KJV corpus by `node verify.js` and again by the build. The quotations in the description above are checked by `node listing/check-listing.js`, which also checks the title length, the 13 tags and the image sizes.
- **Printing at a shop.** Literata is embedded in the PDFs as Type 3 glyphs (a side effect of its variable-font format). Home printers handle this without trouble. Some commercial print shops prefer standard fonts; for a shop run, use the PNG card images instead, or rebuild with static Literata instances.
- **Before selling the printed deck.** The Game Crafter and MakePlayingCards need a tuck box design, which is not built yet. Check each platform's current template and card size before ordering; The Game Crafter's poker card is 825 x 1125 px, the same as our images.
- **Rebuilding.** Edit `content.js`, then run `node products/table-talk/build.js` and `node products/table-talk/listing/build-listing.js`. Both stop if any quotation differs from the corpus.
