Crisis and safety

If you are in immediate danger, call 911.

Red Letter Advisor is a faith reflection tool. It shares words of Jesus from Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John. It is software. When optional Advisor chat is on, replies may be generated by artificial intelligence. You are not talking to a person, a pastor, a therapist, or a crisis counselor.

This product is not a substitute for professional medical, mental-health, or legal care. It is not a crisis service and cannot send help to your location.

Suicide, self-harm, or a mental-health or substance-use crisis (United States and territories)
The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline offers 24/7 support. Call or text 988. Chat: chat.988lifeline.org. Español: llama al 988 y oprime 2; envía AYUDA al 988; chat en español.

988 is a public service. We are not part of 988 or SAMHSA. Official short line (SAMHSA media end cards, posted text still live 11 Sep 2026): “988 offers 24/7 judgment-free support for mental health, substance use, and more.”

Outside the United States — IASP lists local options and is not a hotline: iasp.info/suicidalthoughts. Directory: crisis-centres-helplines.

Abuse or domestic violence — If you are in immediate danger, call 911. In the U.S., the National Domestic Violence Hotline (thehotline.org; we are not affiliated): call 1-800-799-7233, text START to 88788, or chat at thehotline.org.

Digital safety: someone with this device may see this page, your history, and localStorage. Internet activity cannot be fully erased. If it is not safe to use the internet, call 1-800-799-7233 from a safer phone.

If you write about suicide, self-harm, or abuse here, the Advisor stops and shows these resources. We do not continue as if we can talk you through a crisis. We do not contact 911, 988, The Hotline, your family, or the police for you.

Privacy

Operator contact: hello@redletter.app (also used as the Web Push subject). Replace this address when you deploy your own instance.

No account. We do not ask you to register and we do not keep a user profile.

On your device. localStorage holds display preferences, onboarding, locally saved reflections, journal, garden, and similar settings. Anyone with the device can read it. Clearing site data removes it.

Cookies. A first-party quota cookie named rla_qid enforces the free daily Advisor limit without an account. It is not an advertising cookie.

Optional push reminders. If you allow notifications from the installed Home Screen app, the browser gives us a push subscription. We store it only to send the reminders you asked for. Turn notifications off in the app or browser to stop.

Optional AI chat. If you send a message to the Advisor and this instance has an AI key, that text goes to the configured model provider (Anthropic when that key is set) to generate a reply. That vendor’s policy applies. Those messages are not a confidential therapy record and are not covered by HIPAA. Corpus mode (no key) answers from the local verified Gospel library and does not send your question to a model.

Our servers. We may log technical data (IP address, user-agent, time, error codes) and the fact that a safety filter matched. We do not run a separate analytics warehouse. Logs live on the host until that host rotates them. We do not sell your information or use ad pixels.

Waitlist. If you join the Plus waitlist, we store the email you typed on the server (file on the host) so we can write once when Plus exists. Do not submit someone else’s address.

Children. This service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices. Clear this site’s cookies and site data to remove localStorage and the quota cookie. Revoke notification permission to end push. Email the operator for server logs or a waitlist row you can identify. We do not honor a separate Do Not Track switch because we do not run third-party advertising trackers.

California. We describe collection, sources (you, your device, the browser), and purposes (provide the Advisor, enforce the free cap, send opted-in push, waitlist, security) on this page. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in California law. If that changes, this page will say so.
