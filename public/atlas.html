Conscience atlas

Twelve rooms, one first word

The first verse is the one a person on a bad night reads. Each room refuses a sentence that used to wound.

Anxiety & Worry

Take therefore no thought for the morrow…

Matthew 6:34 · KJV

Never leads a veteran or the ashamed. Shame opens on the lost sheep, not tomorrow’s rent.

Grief & Loss

Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.

Matthew 5:4 · KJV

Never contains many mansions. Comfort first, not departure as escape.

Forgiveness

Be ye therefore merciful, as your Father also is merciful.

Luke 6:36 · KJV

Never leads with forgive-not. Affair day is not this room.

Loneliness

I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you.

John 14:18 · KJV

Company, not a technique. Not an orphan story.

Conflict & Relationships

First be reconciled to thy brother…

Matthew 5:23-24 · KJV

Never leads with love your enemies. The first move is small enough for today.

Fear

Fear not, little flock…

Luke 12:32 · KJV

No longer carries Jairus’s “only believe.” A dying child’s parent must not be handed it.

Purpose & Direction

Ye are the light of the world.

Matthew 5:14 · KJV

A job lost is worry. A house lost is suffering. “Lost” alone is not this room.

Faith & Doubt

Blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed.

John 20:29 · KJV

A body not getting better is Suffering, never this room.

Suffering & Pain

Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden…

Matthew 11:28 · KJV

Come first. Tribulation may be third, never the first sentence.

Shame & Guilt

What man of you, having an hundred sheep…

Luke 15:4 · KJV

The one who hit, and the veteran, open here — never “this is not your fault,” never take no thought.

Peace

Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you…

John 14:27 · KJV

Crisis uses this verse, then Come, then the numbered hairs — not the tribulation line from this room’s third passage.

Hope

And ye now therefore have sorrow: but I will see you again…

John 16:22 · KJV

Sorrow is admitted. Joy is not a mood you manufacture.
