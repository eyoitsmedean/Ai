If the room fails

Reach a person

This page does not quote scripture. It does not write a letter. It cannot hand you to a counselor. The numbers below leave the app.

United States — distress

Call or text 988. Chat is a third official channel. You do not have to be suicidal to use it.

988 Call or text chat.988lifeline.org Official 988 chat

Something taken

If you have taken pills or anything else to harm yourself, that is a medical emergency — even if you feel fine. Call 911 if someone collapses, cannot breathe, or has a seizure.

911 Emergency 1-800-222-1222 Poison Control

Violence or abuse

Internet usage can be monitored and is impossible to erase completely. If you are concerned this screen can be seen, call from a safer phone. Clear the browser history after you leave.
1-800-799-7233 National Domestic Violence Hotline Text START to 88788

If you are in immediate danger, call 911.
