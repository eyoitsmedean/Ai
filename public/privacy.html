Red Words — Privacy

Effective 11 September 2026. Operator: Dean Ray.

Red Words stores your last saying, saved sayings, your one honest step, and a dark-mode preference on your device only. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and does not connect to the internet.

Tapping 988 opens your phone’s dialer; Red Words does not place calls, read call history, or access contacts.

No third-party analytics, advertising, or crash SDKs are included. Nothing is shared off the device.

Deleting the app deletes this data. There is no account to delete.

Scripture text is the public-domain World English Bible. Reflections are not Scripture.

This page is the store-listing privacy policy. The same text appears in the app under Settings.
