Privacy · a plain page

What stays on the phone

Red Letter is a reading room for the words Jesus spoke in Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John. It is software, not a person. It is built so that what you write stays with you.

There are no accounts

You never sign in. There is no profile, no email required to read, no password. Your journal, your sittings, your Seven Days, and the words you keep are stored in your browser on this device, and nowhere else, unless you choose to send something.

One sentence

Nothing you write is stored on our server. The server holds the sealed Gospel text and, when a letter is written, forwards your words once and forgets them.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts except the typefaces from Google Fonts.
  • No selling or sharing of anything you write.
  • No reading of your journal by anyone. We cannot; it is not on our servers.
  • No notifications unless you turn the morning reminder on, and then only from your own device.

How your words can be lost, and how to keep them

Because everything lives in your browser, clearing site data or “Begin again” erases it. On iPhone, Safari removes a site’s saved data after seven days of using Safari without opening that site; a room added to the home screen is exempt. In the room, Settings → Keep a copy saves a file you own and can bring back on any device.

Crisis

Red Letter is not therapy, medical care, or pastoral counseling. If you are in danger: call or text 988 in the U.S. (free, 24 hours) or find a local line at findahelpline.com. When the room notices crisis language it shows these lines first.

Children

Red Letter is written for adults and is not directed to children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this page changes, the date below changes. There is no other notice, because there is no account to notify.
