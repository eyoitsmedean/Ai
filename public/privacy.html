Red Words

Privacy

Red Words collects nothing.

  • No account. No sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No network requests. The Gospel sentences ship inside the app and are read from the device.
  • No personal data is collected, stored, transmitted, sold, or shared.

What is written on the device

Three things, local, deletable by removing the app:

  1. A flag recording that the title leaf has been turned once.
  2. The seven-day Word rotation (sentence and citation only) for the home-screen widget. The same seven sentences also ship inside the widget extension.
  3. An optional one-sentence reply from Sit → Respond, keyed to the civil date. It never leaves the device.

Crisis

The About leaf shows the US 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and can open the phone dialer with 988 pre-filled. Red Words does not place the call and does not know whether you did.
