Reach · not a launch

First guest hour

One person. One hour. No ads, no store, no public URL. You sit in the room with them. The screen does not replace you.

If they are in danger: stop the hour. 988 · findahelpline.com. You are the human. The bot is not.

Watch

Before they arrive. Open /ask on your laptop. Open /need on your phone. Open /sit for the silent minute. Charge both. Put 988 on a paper slip on the table. Do not film them without asking.

0–5 · Welcome

Say: this is not church and not therapy. It is one page that only quotes words Jesus spoke. You can leave anytime. Show the cannot-do block on /ask before they type.

5–15 · Sit

Do not start with the chatbot. Open the concordance or /sit. Let them pick a room. Read the saying aloud once. Sit in silence for one full minute. You go first if they freeze.

15–30 · Ask

They type one honest sentence on /ask. You do not coach the wording. If the page stays quiet (off-scope or unmatched), that is a success — praise the silence. If it returns a saying, read it once. Do not explain it.

30–40 · Keep

If they want, sit again, or send a blessing from the folio to one person they name. Not a feed. One person.

40–50 · Limits

Read the colophon together: what this is, which text, why no store. If they ask “is this Jesus?”, you say no. If they ask “can I pay?”, you say His words are not for sale this hour.

50–60 · Close

Ask: do you want this page again, or was once enough? Write their answer on paper. Do not collect an email unless they offer. Thank them. Close the laptop before they leave.

What you will not do

  • Submit the stores. Film an ad. Publish a URL.
  • Let the model keep talking after a crisis line.
  • Pretend the hour is research on them. It is hospitality.

Bearing: C1 · C3 · C5. After the hour, write three lines: what they asked, which saying landed, what you would change. That is the only metric.
