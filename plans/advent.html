Advent · Week 1 · 29 Nov – 5 Dec 2026

The words stay free.

Seven mornings. One thing He said. One breath. One blank line. A missed day is not a debt. This is not for sale.

From the Red Letter Seven already in this repository. Advent Sunday computed: 29 Nov 2026. Folio is parked as cash.

Day 1 · Sunday 29 Nov

Come

Matthew 11:28–29

Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls.

Day one is not a program. It is an invitation. Come as you are — laden, not finished.

What are you carrying that you were never asked to finish tonight?

Day 2 · Monday 30 Nov

Peace

John 14:27

Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.

The world offers a pause. He leaves a gift. You do not have to manufacture calm to receive it.

Where did you try to make peace instead of receiving it?

Day 3 · Tuesday 1 Dec

Light

John 8:12

I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.

Dark seasons are real. He does not deny them. Following is how the next step becomes visible.

What is the next step you can actually see?

Day 4 · Wednesday 2 Dec

Love

John 13:34

A new commandment I give unto you, That ye love one another; as I have loved you, that ye also love one another.

The mark is not an argument. It is how you treat the person next to you today.

One name. One kindness that costs you fifteen minutes or less.

Day 5 · Thursday 3 Dec

Forgive

Matthew 18:21–22

I say not unto thee, Until seven times: but, Until seventy times seven.

Mercy is a way of life, not a single heroic act. One name is enough for this day.

Who gets a quieter sentence from you today?

Day 6 · Friday 4 Dec

Abide

John 15:4–5

Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, except it abide in the vine; no more can ye, except ye abide in me. I am the vine, ye are the branches.

Fruit comes from staying close, not from straining alone. Remain. That is the work.

What would “remain” look like for one hour?

Day 7 · Saturday 5 Dec

Go

Matthew 28:20

Lo, I am with you always, even unto the end of the world.

The last word of the seven is not goodbye. It is presence that does not expire. Go — he goes too.

Where do you go tomorrow, and who goes with you?
